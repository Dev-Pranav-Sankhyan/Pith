OUR ARCHITECTURAL MANIFESTO

The Four Tenets of Pith

01

Strict 5-Story Daily Cap

If everything is important, nothing is. Every day our pipeline processes thousands of primary announcements, benchmark papers, and code commits. We discard 99.8% of noise to deliver exactly five shifts that actually alter systems or moats.

02

Mathematical Falsifiability

Opinions are cheap; calibration is earned. Every single story published on Pith includes an explicit, dated prediction with verifiable resolution criteria. Our Brier score, published on the Forecast Ledger, measures exactly how calibrated we are over time.

03

The Anti-FOMO Lowpass Filter

Half of productivity is knowing what NOT to worry about. Pith provides the "Safely Ignore Today" filter sheet explaining why trending Hacker News or TechCrunch drama won't break your production systems or dilute your competitive moat.

04

Multi-Role Translation

A shift in KV-cache eviction latency means one thing to a systems engineer, another to a founder budgeting GPU reservations, and another to an investor pricing software defensibility. Pith maps each event across four operational lenses.
