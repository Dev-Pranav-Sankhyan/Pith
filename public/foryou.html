PITH FOR YOU

A front page that learns you.

Sign in and read the way you normally do. Every click, every minute you spend reading, every story you skip teaches your feed. No AI and no black box. It runs on transparent maths, and it shows you why each story is there.

  1. Sign in with what you already use. Connect GitHub, X, LinkedIn or Google and day one starts from your interests, not a blank page.
  2. Just read, or listen. It learns from clicks, reading time, skips, saves and shares, and reads Your 5 aloud in your name.
  3. Watch it adapt. Your Taste DNA updates live, and you can see, steer or delete every signal.

Daily News, the Forecast Ledger, the Archive and Tech Patterns stay open to everyone. Only this page needs an account.
